Mathematical data provenance and separate licensing

These four Parquet files match the author's original experiment files byte-for-byte
(SHA-256 and byte counts are in data_manifest.json). Public source versions were
used to recover the same prompts, answers, order, schema and serialization; no
new training examples, normalization choices or alternative benchmark versions
were substituted.
